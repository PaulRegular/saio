# NOAA Alaska sablefish (Stock SMART 10956)

## Assessment unit and run

Stock SMART 10956 is one federal Alaska sablefish population spanning the eastern Bering Sea, Aleutian Islands and Gulf of Alaska. It is separate from the U.S. Pacific Coast assessment. The latest full production assessment located is the 2024 update, author-recommended Model 23.5. The final 2025 SAFE catch-report chapter says that no assessment was conducted in 2025 and that the 2024 model was rolled forward for specifications.

The final 2024 SAFE report, model input, control and template files, fitted output, and selected report tables are cached locally under `source_cache/discovery_noaa_alaska_sablefish_2024_2025/`. The versioned manifest is `provenance/discovery_noaa_alaska_sablefish_2024_2025/sources.csv`; it records source URLs, retrieval dates, pinned GitHub revision where applicable, file sizes, and SHA-256 checksums.

## Inputs captured

The assessment tracks female and male fish at ages 2–31+, with age 31 as the plus group. The input file supplies annual fixed-gear and trawl catch biomass in tonnes for 1960–2024, multiple annual survey and fishery indices with their reported standard errors, and composition likelihood inputs. The database retains the native catch, index, and composition scales; it does not reconstruct catch numbers-at-age.

The source supplies age compositions for the U.S. fixed-gear fishery (1999–2023), NOAA domestic longline survey (1996–2023), Japanese–U.S. cooperative longline survey (1981, 1983, 1985, 1987, 1989, 1991, and 1993), and Gulf of Alaska trawl survey (1984, 1987, 1996, and 1999). Each spans ages 2–31+. The input's relative likelihood sample-size weights are 20 for the first three series and 30 for the Gulf of Alaska trawl series; they are not raw fish counts. The final run assigns iterative likelihood weights 0.8261, 3.7922, and 1.3168 to the fixed-gear, domestic longline, and cooperative longline age compositions, respectively; the supplied Gulf of Alaska trawl age composition has zero likelihood weight. Raw fish age/length sample counts from the SAFE sample-size workbook are stored separately where the table reports them. The workbook filename says Table 3.4 while its embedded heading says Table 3.8; the database preserves the source workbook reference and this numbering discrepancy is noted here.

The input also includes size compositions by length for the U.S. fixed-gear fishery, Japanese longline fishery, U.S. trawl fishery, Japanese trawl survey, NOAA domestic longline survey, Japanese–U.S. cooperative longline survey, and Gulf of Alaska trawl survey. Length-bin midpoints are 41–99 cm in 2 cm increments. The source's Japanese cooperative longline survey length vectors both carry the label “Male”; their ordering was checked against the fitted output object, which identifies the first as male and second as female. The database records the verified sex assignments and documents the mislabeled second vector.

Fixed biological vectors include female maturity-at-age and sex-specific weight-at-age for ages 2–31+. The input file carries alternative blocks in addition to those used in the run. The 2024 control file specifies one active weight block and one active maturity block; the source vectors are retained with block identity, and inactive alternatives are not interpreted as assessment-varying biology. Natural mortality is estimated as one scalar with a prior, not as age- or sex-specific mortality. The fitted output reports a constant M surface of 0.114303 per year.

The input contains both relative population weight (RPW) and relative population number (RPN) series. Domestic and cooperative longline RPW survey series are retained but have zero final likelihood weight; the RPN series and selected fishery CPUE and Gulf of Alaska trawl biomass indices have final weights of 0.448. Corresponding standard errors are stored with each index series. The model reports annual indices through 2023, but neither the report nor input file supplies fractional within-year survey timing. No survey index exists for 2024. Timing is therefore recorded as unreported. Survey age compositions and aggregate indices are retained separately; no age-specific survey abundance index has been reconstructed. The final index and composition weights are recorded as assumptions in the database.

## Outputs captured

The pinned native output object contains female and male numbers-at-age for 1960–2024, annual age-2 recruitment, spawning biomass, total biomass, and the fitted M surface. Total abundance is derived by summing the female and male numbers across ages 2–31+; no uncertainty is available for that derived total. Table 3.8 reports rounded 95% MCMC interval endpoints for recruitment through 2023 and for SSB and total biomass through 2024. Recruitment estimates in the native object extend to 2024, but the report table has no 2024 recruitment interval or point entry; no interval is supplied for that year. Numeric F-at-age output was not located; the report's fully selected F series is not an age-specific mortality surface.

## Curation status and remaining gaps

The source data and outputs are imported under `noaa_alaska_sablefish_2024`. Database validation and source-specific tests check the years, ages, units, likelihood weights, sample counts, reported intervals, model-output agreement, and provenance. The assessment remains marked partial because fractional survey timing and numeric F-at-age were not located, and some regional or fleet details are only represented in the model's already-aggregated likelihood inputs. These gaps should remain explicit in any later translation.
