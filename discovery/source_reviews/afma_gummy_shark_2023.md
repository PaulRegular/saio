# AFMA Gummy Shark, 2023 assessment

## Current stock unit and run

AFMA manages Gummy Shark (*Mustelus antarcticus*) in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery. The 2023 assessment is the latest detailed assessment linked from AFMA's species page. The official production model is Bridge 17, selected by SharkRAG in October 2023, and uses data through 2022.

The model has three components: Bass Strait, South Australia, and Tasmania. These are retained as regional components of one assessment case.

## Recoverable source data

Appendix A, Tables 14-16 report annual total removals by gear and component for 1927-2022, in tonnes. The official Bridge 17 model uses seven fleets: shallow line, four gillnet mesh sizes, trawl, and deep line. Danish seine is listed in the source tables for some runs but is not used in Bridge 17. The 2,016 imported catch rows retain the seven fleet histories for Bass Strait, South Australia, and Tasmania, including source-reported zeroes. The report labels the annual series by model year but does not state calendar-year or fleet-specific fishing-year boundaries.

The model also uses aggregate gillnet, trawl, and shallow-line CPUE, length frequencies, age compositions, conditional age-at-length data, and tag-recapture observations. The report describes their coverage and use but does not provide the full numerical observation arrays. Age-composition inputs are described for selected gillnet fleets from 1986-2008; conditional age-at-length data are described for 1995-2022. Age data before 1995 are available as age compositions but the underlying paired age-length records are unavailable to the assessment authors.

## Assessment assumptions and outputs

Bridge 17 models the three components separately, with stock-specific recruitment and CPUE power parameters, and shared adult natural mortality, gear selectivities, and productivity parameters. Sexes are modeled separately with fixed sex-specific growth. Adult M is estimated jointly; juvenile M is calculated to give equilibrium in the absence of fishing. The report's Table 5 gives an adult M estimate of 0.18 per year.

The model uses pup production as a proxy for spawning biomass. Table 5 reports the terminal 2022 pup-production depletion as 50% for Bass Strait, 63% for South Australia, and 69% for Tasmania. The same table reports B0, MSYR, depletion in 1973, effort-saturation parameters, and negative log-likelihood values. These selected point estimates are imported without uncertainty intervals. The source does not state the units or biological basis for B0, or the effort scale for the saturation parameter; those values are retained with the limitation recorded. Numeric annual population surfaces are shown only in figures, not as tabulated values.

## Screening decision

The assessment has partial source coverage because it provides numerical annual catch-removal series and selected fitted summary outputs. Age-input eligibility remains unresolved: the assessment uses age compositions and conditional age-at-length data, but the numerical arrays, CPUE series, and age-specific biological vectors were not found in the reviewed report. No values are reconstructed from plots. The report does not provide age-specific N, F, M, recruitment, or annual SSB output tables, and pup production depletion is not relabelled as SSB.
