# Icelandic ling, Division 5.a (2026)

**Review date:** 2026-11-14
**ICES stock code:** lin.27.5a  
**Scientific name:** *Molva molva*
**Assessment year:** 2026  
**Terminal fitted year:** 2025

## Assessment and inputs recovered

The 2026 MFRI report describes the current Icelandic ling assessment as an age-based SAM assessment. ICES Standard Graphs identifies the advice assessment as lin.27.5a. The 2023 ICES Stock Annex describes modeled ages 2–12+ and fixed natural mortality of 0.15 per year.

The MFRI supplementary tables page provides numeric age series that had previously been available only in report figures. Table 5 gives catch numbers-at-age for ages 3–12+ in 1979–2025, in thousands of fish. Age 2 is not tabulated and is left missing. Table 6 gives commercial catch weights-at-age, and Table 8 gives stock weights-at-age, both for ages 2–12+ in 1979–2025 and in grams per fish. The separate SSB-weights table is empty; stock weights are not treated as spawning-stock weights. Table 9 gives maturity proportions for ages 2–12+ in 1979–2025. Age 12 is the plus group.

Tables 10–12 give age-specific indices for three surveys:

- IS-SMB spring groundfish survey: ages 2–12+, 1985–2025 (451 values).
- IS-SMH autumn groundfish survey: ages 2–12+, 2000–2025 (280 values); six blank cells remain absent.
- IS-SMN gillnet survey: ages 2–12+, 2002–2025 (256 values); eight blank cells remain absent.

The survey table captions describe the indices as “in numbers,” but do not state a multiplier or absolute count unit. Values are retained on their reported index scale, without rescaling. The report identifies survey months or seasons (March, October, and early April), but the exact within-year sampling fractions used by the assessment remain unavailable. The source tables do not separate catch or biological values by sex or fleet.

The ICES source-data table provides aggregate landings, catches and discards in tonnes for 1979–2025. The 2025 total catch of 9,791 tonnes agrees with the reported value. The fixed M vector for ages 2–12+ is imported from the Stock Annex.

## Outputs recovered

The ICES Standard Graphs source-data table provides age-2 recruitment in thousands of fish, SSB and total biomass in tonnes, and Fbar for ages 8–11 per year. Published low and high limits are retained for those aggregate outputs. Values through 2025 are historical assessment outputs; selected 2026 aggregate values are advice projections.

MFRI supplementary Table 14 reports stock numbers at ages 2–12+ for 1979–2026. The 2026 values are an advice projection. Table 15 reports fishing mortality at ages 2–12+ for 1979–2025. These age-specific point estimates have no reported uncertainty in the table. No age-specific confidence intervals or standard errors are entered.

## Completeness

The 2026 production assessment, aggregate catch series, fixed M, age-specific input series, aggregate outputs and age-specific N/F point surfaces are represented. Input completeness remains partial because survey index multipliers and exact sampling times are unresolved and the source provides no SSB-specific weight table. Reported output point estimates are complete for the extracted series; age-specific uncertainty is unavailable.

Source HTML files are cached locally. The original report and ICES sources are listed in `provenance/discovery_ices_ling_5a_2026/sources.csv`; the newly recovered supplementary table page is listed in `provenance/discovery_ices_ling_5a_2026/supplementary_sources.csv`, with retrieval date, URL, byte size and SHA-256 checksum.
