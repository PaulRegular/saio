# Southern Australia Australian Sardine: 2025 source screen

## Stock and assessment

The latest detailed report located is Bailleul et al. (2025), SARDI Research Report Series 1276, for the Southern Australia stock of Australian Sardine (Sardinops sagax). The South Australian Sardine Fishery is represented in SardEst as one area, fleet and stock. The 2025 assessment is the latest detailed production assessment found in the official PIRSA source pages reviewed on 9 November 2026.

The report describes an annual, single-sex, age-structured model. Its input families include commercial catch, commercial age compositions and DEPM spawning-biomass observations. It does not include a CPUE likelihood.

## Source material described

The assessment report describes annual commercial catch and age-composition data, DEPM spawning-biomass observations, age-based biology, and model estimates of spawning biomass, total biomass, recruitment and mortality. Age compositions and annual model results are presented graphically in parts of the report; the numerical input and output series were not found as a separate machine-readable file.

A separate PIRSA report documents the 2025 DEPM spawning-biomass estimate. It is an aggregate survey quantity, not an age-specific survey index. The sources reviewed did not provide a public native SardEst input/output bundle or a machine-readable age-composition series.

## Source availability and curation decision

The official sources are the [2025 SardEst assessment](https://pir.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/496793/Stock-assessment-of-Australian-Sardine-Sardinops-sagax-off-South-Australia-2025.pdf), the [2025 DEPM report](https://pir.sa.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/491381/20251128-Sardine-Spawning-Biomass-2025-FINAL-V2.pdf), and the [PIRSA Sardine Fishery page](https://pir.sa.gov.au/fishing-and-aquaculture/commercial-fishing/commercial-fisheries/sardine-fishery).

The official reports' copyright notices restrict electronic storage without permission. Direct PDF retrieval in an earlier source-cache attempt also returned an automated browser challenge. No PDFs or checksums have therefore been placed in the local source cache, and no report values have been transcribed into the canonical database. The source cache folder remains empty.

This targeted source-led candidate is screened with age-input eligibility unresolved. No canonical stock, assessment, input, assumption or output rows have been created. Revisit if written permission or a suitable publicly licensed machine-readable/native source becomes available.
