# NOAA BSAI blackspotted and rougheye rockfish (2024)

Review date: 2026-11-12. NOAA Stock SMART unit 11382 is the Bering Sea/Aleutian Islands complex of blackspotted rockfish (*Sebastes melanostictus*) and rougheye rockfish (*S. aleutianus*). The December 2024 SAFE is the latest accepted production update located for this unit. It retains the accepted 2022 Aleutian Islands (AI) age-structured model with updated observations through 2024. The eastern Bering Sea (EBS) component is assessed separately with a Tier 5 random-effects survey-biomass method. A 2026 assessment document was posted for review at the November 16-20, 2026 Plan Team meeting; because that meeting had not occurred by this review date, those pre-meeting materials were not used.

## Source and assessment coverage

The official source is the [2024 BSAI SAFE chapter](https://www.npfmc.org/wp-content/PDFdocuments/SAFE/2024/BSAIrougheye.pdf), cached locally under `source_cache/discovery_noaa_bsai_rougheye_2024/`. The tracked manifest is `provenance/discovery_noaa_bsai_rougheye_2024/sources.csv`; it records the source URL, retrieval date, byte size, and SHA-256 checksum. The report states that the accepted AI model converged with a successful Hessian inversion and maximum gradient component of 9.1e-6. It describes the assessment as the same model structure as the accepted 2022 assessment, updated with new data.

The age-structured AI model covers years 1977-2024, ages 3-44 and an age-45+ accumulator, with recruitment at age 3. Table 14.25 also reports 2025 biomass and SSB forecasts. The model’s total biomass is ages 3+, SSB is mature female biomass ages 3+, and recruitment is age-3 numbers. The published age-specific population series applies only to the AI component; the EBS Tier 5 component has no age-specific population surface in this source.

## Imported source inputs

- **Catch:** Table 14.3 reports BSAI catch in metric tons for 1977-2024, with early foreign, joint-venture, and domestic reporting sectors separated by EBS and AI areas where reported. The 2024 value is estimated through October 5. Table 14.4 reports WAI, CAI, EAI, and EBS catch for 1994-2024. The imported Table 14.4 area series are retained as supporting spatial detail; their components are not additional age-model catch likelihoods and are not added to the Table 14.3 BSAI total.
- **Fishery compositions:** Table 14.12 reports rounded length proportions for 1979, 1990, 1992-1993, 2003, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, and 2022, at 12-49 cm and 50+. Table 14.13 reports rounded age proportions for 2004-2005, 2007-2009, 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, and 2019-2021 and 2023, at ages 3-44 and 45+. First-stage sample sizes are represented by source haul counts from Table 14.10 (lengths) and Table 14.11 (otoliths read). The report specifies that first-stage counts are numbers of hauls; effective sample sizes are not inferred.
- **AI survey compositions:** Table 14.17 reports rounded age proportions for 1991, 1994, 1997, 2000, 2002, 2004, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, and 2022, at ages 3-44 and 45+. First-stage haul counts from Table 14.16 are retained. Table 14.18 gives a 2024 AI survey length composition for 12-49 cm and 50+. Its values are embedded as a page image and absent from text extraction; they were transcribed from the rendered report page. Table 14.15 reports 2,280 length samples from 112 AI survey hauls, but the report does not identify whether those counts are the likelihood sample size for Table 14.18, so the composition's `sample_size` is left blank.
- **Survey biomass:** Table 14.14 reports AI subarea and total survey biomass estimates in metric tons with CVs, plus separate EBS slope survey estimates. The AI total series covers 1991, 1994, 1997, 2000, 2002, 2004, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2022, and 2024. The report's model-data summary omits the 2016 value that appears in Table 14.14; that observation is preserved with the discrepancy noted and its likelihood use unresolved. Subarea estimates are components of the total, not independent aggregate series. EBS slope observations belong to the separate Tier 5 component, not the AI age-structured model. Fractional within-year survey timing is not stated.
- **Weight and maturity:** Table 14.19 reports a population weight-at-age vector in grams, ages 3-44 and 45+. The report estimates it outside the population model from AI survey growth (Linf 51.43 cm, K 0.06 per year, t0 -3.30 years) and the length-weight relationship W = 6.46e-6 L^3.24. A separate year-specific fishery weight-at-age vector is described by the model but not tabulated. Table 14.19 also reports the predicted maturity-at-age curve. Maturity parameters are estimated inside the assessment and are retained separately from the predicted curve; raw biological sample data are not included.
- **Mortality and model settings:** M is a scalar estimated with a lognormal prior mean of 0.045 per year and log-scale SD 0.05; the reported estimate is 0.05, with its standard deviation rounded to 0.00. AI survey catchability has a prior mean of 1.0 and log-scale SD 0.05; the reported estimate is 1.04 with SD 0.05. The report gives likelihood weights of 50 for catch, 1 for the survey index, and 0.1 for fishing-mortality deviations. Composition second-stage weights use McAllister-Ianelli weighting. Spawning occurs at 0.17 of the year. The age-reading CV is 0.121 from 2,341 double readings (1986-2017); the numerical age-error matrix and age-length conversion matrix are not tabulated.

Rounded composition values are retained as published and are not renormalized. No age-specific catch numbers are constructed: the report provides catch biomass and separate compositions, but does not publish the fishery weight-at-age data needed to make an exact biomass-to-numbers expansion. No survey timing fraction or model-input scaling is inferred.

## Imported official outputs and remaining gaps

- Table 14.26 gives AI numbers-at-age for 1977-2024, in millions of fish, ages 3-44 and 45+. Age-specific uncertainty is not tabulated. Total abundance is derived by summing the published age groups; its uncertainty remains blank because joint covariance is unavailable.
- Table 14.25 gives total biomass and SSB estimates for 1977-2024 and 2025 forecast point estimates. Hessian CVs are reported for the historical rows; natural-scale SEs are calculated as estimate times the source CV and the reported CV is preserved in row notes. The 2025 forecast has no reported CV.
- Table 14.25 gives age-3 recruitment, in thousands of fish, for 1977-2018 only. Historical CVs are converted to natural-scale SEs in the same documented way. No recruitment point estimates are reported for later years in this table.
- Table 14.23 gives scalar M and AI survey q estimates. M's reported SD rounds to 0.00, so no zero SE is recorded. Maturity curve parameters are retained on their source model scales.
- The reviewed source does not provide fitted F-at-age, catch-at-age numbers, the full age-error or age-length conversion matrices, raw maturity observations, or the year-specific fishery weight-at-age vector. These remain explicit gaps; assessment statuses are partial.
