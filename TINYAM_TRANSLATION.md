# Downstream tinyAM translations

The maintained tinyAM translation protocol is [TINYAM_TRANSLATION.md in tinyAM](https://github.com/PaulRegular/tinyAM/blob/main/analysis/comp_assessments/TINYAM_TRANSLATION.md). Translation settings, review tiers, fit diagnostics and dashboards belong there. Source curation in SAIO follows PROTOCOL.md and DATABASE_STRUCTURE.md and does not require a tinyAM translation.
