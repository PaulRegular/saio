# NAFO beaked redfish in Division 3M, 2024 assessment

## Assessment and sources

The latest recoverable full age-based assessment remains the 2024 XSA for the combined beaked redfish stock (*Sebastes mentella* and *S. fasciatus*) in Division 3M. The 2024 assessment was used for advice for 2025–2026. NAFO's June 2026 Council report scheduled new 3M redfish advice for September 2026 and states that it was deferred to that meeting. As of the 2026 advice page and Council-report listings checked on 2026-11-05, no September 2026 redfish assessment report or replacement production XSA was publicly listed. The assessment applies to beaked redfish only; golden redfish (*S. norvegicus*) is excluded.

The separately listed NAFO SCR Doc. 24/032, “An update assessment of beaked redfish in Division 3M,” has no download link in the NAFO catalogue. Its expected PDF URL, `https://www.nafo.int/Portals/0/PDFs/sc/2024/scr24-032.pdf`, returned 404 when checked on 2026-11-05. A related 2026 paper, SCR Doc. 26/010, estimates a candidate biomass limit reference point using output from the 2021 assessment; it is not a new production XSA and the candidate value is not entered as an adopted reference point or as a 2026 assessment output. The 2026 catalogue also lists SCR Doc. 26/027, “Age length keys comparison for beaked redfish,” but no download link was provided and the expected PDF URL returned 404 on 2026-11-04. The available Council report provides the 2024 model summary, assumptions and short-term projection table. Cached source files and checksums are listed in `provenance/nafo_redfish_3m_2024/manifest.csv`; cached PDFs remain local-only in the ignored `source_cache/` directory.

## Inputs and assumptions

The accessible sources do not provide the annual age-specific commercial catch, EU survey indices, weight-at-age or maturity-at-age matrices used in the 2024 XSA. SCR Doc. 24/024 confirms that the 2024 assessment selected a species-combined age-length key and maturity ogive pooled from 2020–2022 because 2023 survey age readings and maturity data were unavailable. This selection is recorded in the canonical assumptions. The report provides age-length summaries and plots of standardized survey abundance by age, but not the complete numerical XSA input matrices; those plots are not digitized. There is a source wording discrepancy in the historical age-length-key coverage: SCS Doc. 24/16 describes *S. mentella* keys for 1988–2017, while SCR Doc. 24/024 describes keys for 1990–2007 and 2009–2018 and a synthetic 2008 key. The documents do not provide enough numerical matrices to resolve this discrepancy or reconstruct the assessment inputs.

The assessment summary reports STACFIS estimated beaked redfish catch for 2015–2023 in thousands of tonnes. Those values are based on species proportions in observed catch, and are preserved as a separate total-catch series; they are not a substitute for the missing age-specific catches. Combined catch and TAC values for all three redfish species are not attributed to this beaked-redfish stock.

The 2024 XSA used ages 4–19+, the EU Flemish Cap summer survey, and the documented historical natural-mortality settings. From 2015 onward the assessment used M=0.10; earlier periods included higher M assumptions, including 0.4 tuned to ages 4–6 in 2006–2010 and applied to all ages in 2009–2010. Catchability was independent of stock size and age-independent from age 16; no tapered time weights were used. Terminal survivors were not shrunk to mean F, oldest-age survivors were not shrunk, and a minimum standard error of 0.5 was imposed at the last true age.

## Outputs and completeness

Table 7.1 in SCS Doc. 24/16 reports beginning-of-year 2024–2027 SSB, yield and TAC under seven scenarios. The shared 2024 baseline values and 2025 SSB (common to all scenarios) are recorded once. Scenario-specific 2026–2027 SSB, 2025–2026 yield, and 2025–2026 TAC are recorded with their source scenario in the outputs table. The 2025–2027 SSB intervals are central 80% intervals, not 95% intervals.

Historical annual SSB, numbers-at-age, F-at-age, M-at-age, and age-specific XSA diagnostics are shown only in figures or are not available in the accessible material; they are not digitized. No accepted limit reference point exists. F0.1=0.0675 is a candidate reference point, while Fmax is considered too uncertain and is not accepted.

Assumptions, inputs and outputs are **partial**. The imported numerical data comprise the published beaked catch totals and the complete short-term projection table; important age-specific model inputs and fitted historical outputs remain unavailable. The 2024 SCR 24/032 assessment report is an explicit source-access blocker, not evidence that the underlying data do not exist.

This review records source facts and gaps only; it contains no downstream model settings or comparison results.
