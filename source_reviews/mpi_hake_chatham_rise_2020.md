# Chatham Rise hake (HAK 4 and part of HAK 1)

Reviewed 2026-11-14 from English-language Fisheries New Zealand reports.

The May 2026 Plenary, Hake section 4.2, still reports the 2020 assessment
for all of HAK 4 and the Chatham Rise portion of HAK 1 north of Otago
Peninsula. This is distinct from the Sub-Antarctic HAK 1 and West Coast
South Island HAK 7 stocks already in SAIO. FAR 2021/22 documents the
current assessment. The 2026 catch-at-age update is a monitoring report.

## Verified source coverage

- FAR 2021/22 Tables 2 and 7 report the January core-trawl biomass indices
  and sampling CVs and the east and west fishery catches in tonnes. The
  terminal model year (2020) uses an assumed catch of 436 t based on recent
  catches and a survey observation from January 2020. Model years run
  September to August and are labelled by the year in which they end.
- The CASAL 2.30 base case pools the sexes, models ages 1–30+, and uses
  survey and east/west fishery age proportions. No CPUE series is used in
  the base case. Tables 1, 5 and 6 document fixed annual M of 0.19, the
  growth, length–weight and maturity relationships, the annual schedule,
  and effective sample sizes. The model places the survey after all
  mortality in the first time step, which includes 0.42 of annual natural
  mortality and all fishing mortality. This is a model setting rather than
  a fractional calendar sampling date.
- FAR 2021/15 Table 7 gives expanded January 2020 survey numbers-at-age
  and CVs by sex. FAR 2019/12 gives corresponding values for 2018. The
  historical fitted age proportions are shown in figures. The full
  numerical commercial and survey age arrays and native CASAL files were
  not located in the reviewed public sources.
- FAR 2021/22 Table 10 reports base-case terminal spawning biomass of
  18,150 t, with a 95% Bayesian credible interval of 13,204–27,258 t.
  The report plots historical biomass, relative year-class strength and
  exploitation. Annual official N and F at age were not located as
  numerical tables.

This stock has recoverable data for a partial source import. Its eligibility
for an age-data batch is unresolved pending the full historical composition
series. It remains in discovery.
